FolioPair does not collect your documents, notes, or usage analytics.

Who provides this app

FolioPair is provided by zongmeng zhao. This policy covers version 1.0 of the iPhone and iPad app.

Documents and notes

Imported PDFs, quotations, notes, bookmarks, and reading positions are stored in the app’s library on your device. FolioPair does not operate a backend for these files or upload them to a developer-controlled service.

No tracking or advertising

The app has no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does not request an advertising identifier, and does not require an account.

Files, exports, and backups

You choose files to import using Apple’s document picker. Exports are sent only to destinations you choose through the system share sheet. Those destinations have their own privacy policies. Your operating system may include the app’s library in device backups according to your settings.

Support

If you contact the developer, the information you voluntarily provide is used to answer your request. Do not send confidential documents unnecessarily. Contact richardzhao1618@gmail.com.

Apple services

Apple processes App Store purchases and may provide diagnostics according to your device and Apple account settings. Apple’s processing is governed by its own policies.

Deleting information

Removing a document inside FolioPair moves it to Recently Removed so it can be restored. Deleting the app removes its local library. Export anything you want to keep first. Copies in backups or export destinations must be managed through those services.

This support website

This website contains static help pages and no developer-added analytics, advertising, or contact forms. Its hosting provider may process technical request information needed to serve the pages. This website is separate from the app’s local document library.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date. For privacy questions, use the support contact on the support page.

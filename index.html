Support / Version 1.0

A little help, then back to reading.

How do investigations work?

In Research, create a question and add claims. Open Sources to attach or import PDFs. Read a source, select text and tap Use selected passage, then assign it to a claim as Supports, Challenges or Context. Keep the source excerpt separate from your interpretation.

What does the matrix tell me?

It shows the evidence you collected for each source and claim. Coverage check identifies claims with opposing evidence, claims with no evidence and support without a recorded challenge. It does not assess truth or source reliability. On smaller displays, scroll the matrix horizontally.

How do I export a research brief?

Open an investigation, choose Brief and tap Edit. Write a working conclusion, unresolved questions and a next step. Export produces Markdown containing your claims, passages, interpretations and numbered references. It does not embed the source PDFs.

Where can I find archived investigations?

Use the filter menu on the Research home screen to show archived questions. Open an investigation and choose Restore investigation from its options menu.

How do I read and take notes together?

Open a document from the library. On a wide screen, the PDF and notebook appear beside each other. On a narrow screen, use Read and Notes. iPhone Duo adjusts the paired workspace around its fold’s reserved region. Focus reading is available from the workspace menu.

How do I save a quotation?

Touch and hold selectable text in the PDF, adjust the selection, then tap Save excerpt. Add your thought and tap Save. Scanned pages without a text layer require OCR in another app before text can be selected or searched.

Which PDFs can I import?

Unlocked PDFs up to 100 MB. Password-protected documents must be unlocked before importing. Very large or complex documents may take longer to open and search.

Where are my documents and research stored?

In FolioPair’s local library on your device. The app has no account, document server, or cloud sync. System backups may include this library according to your device settings. Export your notes and research briefs regularly, keep copies of original PDFs, and export before deleting the app or changing devices.

Can I restore a removed document?

Open Help & settings, find Recently Removed, and tap Restore. This preserves its notes. Removing the app itself deletes the local library and is different from removing a document inside FolioPair.

Does FolioPair include OCR, handwriting, or PDF editing?

Version 1.0 includes cross-document evidence investigations, research briefs, PDF reading and linked typed notes. It does not include OCR, handwriting, editing the PDF itself, or cloud sync. Notes export as Markdown; you can also share the original PDF.
